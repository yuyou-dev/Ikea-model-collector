# Legal and usage boundaries

Read this reference before starting any acquisition task and carry its warnings
into the collection handoff.

## Required position

- Treat Ikea-model-collector as an independent, unofficial project. It is not
  affiliated with, sponsored by, approved by, or endorsed by IKEA or Inter IKEA
  Systems B.V.
- Treat IKEA names, trademarks, product designs, models, textures, product
  imagery, and other site material as third-party material. Apache-2.0 applies
  to this Skill's original code and documentation, not to IKEA material.
- Use the workflow only for personal research, learning, and non-commercial
  experimentation. It does not grant commercial-use, redistribution, public
  hosting, mirroring, sale, sublicensing, or dataset-publication rights.
- Never commit a captured model or collection to this Skill repository. Keep it
  in the user's ignored local collection root.
- Do not perform unattended or large-scale scraping. Keep tasks user-directed,
  bounded, serial, and low-frequency.

## Terms acknowledgement

Require the user to check the current terms for the IKEA locale they use. Store
the locale, terms URL, acknowledgment timestamp, and product page URL in the
collection. Do not proceed if acknowledgment is missing.

At the initial release, the IKEA U.S. Terms & Conditions state an effective date
of May 1, 2026. They describe limited non-commercial personal use of a single
copy of site material and restrict copying, distribution, and scraping without
written permission. Do not describe that language as blanket authorization for
3D-model capture or reuse. Link the current source:

https://www.ikea.com/us/en/customer-service/terms-conditions/

When the requested action may exceed personal research, stop and tell the user
to obtain permission from the relevant rights holder.

## Handoff wording

State all of the following in every completed collection:

1. The files were captured locally by the user-directed workflow.
2. The collection includes IKEA-controlled third-party material.
3. It is intended only for personal research, learning, and non-commercial
   experimentation.
4. It must not be redistributed, sold, mirrored, or publicly hosted without
   permission.
5. The user remains responsible for the current site terms and applicable law.
